Connect your cTrader accounts (one-time setup, about 5 minutes)

Already set up on another computer?

  1. Create a free cTrader Open API app. Go to openapi.ctrader.com/apps, log in with your cTrader ID and click Add new app. Give it any name. In Redirect URIs, add exactly this address: cTrader reviews new apps before they work. This can take a day, so wait until the app shows as Active.
  2. Paste your app's Client ID and Secret. On the same page, click Credentials next to your app.

  3. Connect. You'll log in at cTrader and allow read-only access to your accounts.
